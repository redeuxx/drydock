    The service the rest of the stack is there for: the one no other service depends on (by depends_on), preferring one that binds a host port. It is named under each stack.

    Switching the stack's tag moves the main service and its replicas. Images of the same app from the same publisher on the same tag move with it (immich-server and immich-machine-learning), so an app and its workers stay on one version. A database or a cache keeps its own tag, and when the main service follows latest, or is one of Docker Hub's official images, it moves alone.
